At Popoola-Taiwo LP

We are more than just legal practitioners; we are your partners in navigating the complexities of the law with precision, dedication, and innovation. Established in 2018 and headquartered in Victoria Island, Lagos, with a branch in Abuja, we are a full-service commercial law firm offering bespoke legal solutions across diverse practice areas.

Driven by the principles of excellence, integrity, and responsiveness, we are committed to understanding the unique needs of our clients and providing tailored strategies that deliver value, mitigate risks, and seize opportunities.

Who we are

Popoola-Taiwo LP is a dynamic, forward-thinking law firm built on a foundation of technical expertise, collaborative teamwork, and a deep understanding of the evolving legal landscape in Nigeria and beyond.

We serve a wide range of clients, including financial institutions, multinational corporations, startups, and government agencies, providing sound legal advice and solutions that address their specific needs.

From facilitating complex maritime transactions to protecting intellectual property rights, resolving disputes, and advising on corporate governance, we are equipped to handle legal challenges with a practical, solution-oriented approach.
