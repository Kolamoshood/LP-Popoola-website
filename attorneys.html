Meet The Team

Shehu Popoola-Taiwo

Founder

Shehu Popoola-Taiwo is the founding partner of Popoola-Taiwo LP. He is an experienced legal practitioner with over Ten Years of Practice Experience under his belt. He is a seasoned maritime enthusiast, a dispute resolution specialist, and a human and resources managing strategist whose core areas of interest include, Maritime law and Alternative Dispute Resolution.

Shehu Popoola-Taiwo is also the founder of Court 86, Africa’s First Online Dispute Resolution Platform which was shortlisted by The Hague Institute for innovation of Law as one of the world’s best Justice Innovation at the HIL Justice Accelerator 2018. Asides being the Chairman of Youngship Nigeria, Shehu Currently serves as the International Maritime Organization (IMO) working group’s Executive Coordinator, where he is able to use his skills to influence and contribute immensely to the development of Maritime globally.

He is also on the verge of Obtaining a Masters of Sciences Degree in Maritime Administration and Management in the Prestigious University of Lagos. Shehu is a member of The Nigerian Bar Association, London Court of Arbitration, International Council of Commercial Arbbitration, amongst others.
