Maritime Law

Our team consists of highly skilled and knowledgeable people who are very passionate about contributing to the development of the maritime industry.

Our Associates’ versatility and expertise fortifies us with the technical know-how

and business cum legal advisory potential reuired to render our services to a wide range of clients in virtually all the known areas of the maritime industry at a jet speed.

The firm has rendered services to various maritime companies which includes Brawal Shipping Nigeria Ltd., Joatelim Logistics Ltd., Lister Flour Ltd., Interocean Marine Services Ltd., amongst others.

Dispute Resolution, ADR & ODR

As one of our core practice areas, our team’s resilience, hard work and thirst for obtaining justice for our clients has put us in the forefront of Litigation.

We counsel clients on the best and cost-effective approach to resolve their conflicts. Our team is highly skilled in advocacy, negotiation of settlement, and alternative dispute resolution that are result oriented and client focused.

With our Founding Partner as an Online Dispute Resolution enthusiast, we also undertake Online Dispute Resolution proceedings, including cross border dispute resolution. We have represented our clients in several Criminal, Civil and Commercial disputes that includes employment and Labour disputes, Divorce and Child Custody Proceedings, Land disputes, debt recovery, maritime and freight forwarding disputes.

Fundamental Right Enforcement

Our Associates are highly skilled in ensuring the cessation of a right that is been infringed upon, and the compensation of a right that has been infringed on.

We have taken up numerous cases on Fundamental Rights Enforcement, in which we have demonstrated the understanding that nothing can totally compensate a loss suffered or a right infringed upon.

We have however helped our clients secure who have been victims of breach of fundamental human rights the best awards and damages to mitigate the hardship suffered from the breach of their rights.

Commercial Law & Corporate Practice

Intellectual Property Law

Our Intellectual Property Law practice is dedicated to helping creatives, innovators, and businesses protect their works, ideas, and brands while maximizing their value.

From artists and musicians to tech entrepreneurs and corporate clients, we provide tailored legal solutions that safeguard intellectual assets, prevent exploitation, and create opportunities for growth. Our expertise extends to protecting intellectual works through copyright, trademark, patent, and industrial design registrations, giving our clients the legal foundation to assert ownership and enforce their rights when disputes arise. Whether through negotiation, arbitration, or litigation, we are committed to achieving timely and effective resolutions that protect our clients’ reputations and intellectual property.
